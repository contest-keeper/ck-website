# E.5 Dashboard views for matching and alerts

**Depends on:** E.4 · **Next:** F.1

## Why
Organizers see at a glance which submissions are verified and which are suspicious.

## Context
- Builds on the C.4 dashboard. Colour is never the only signal (projector, colour-blind users):
  use labels and icons too.

## Small steps
- [ ] **E.5.1** Submissions view: platform submission ↔ stick event, status (matched / unmatched / pending).
- [ ] **E.5.2** Alert detail: what happened, evidence (events, times, stick, handle), acknowledge and note.
- [ ] **E.5.3** Per-team summary for the end of the contest (export to CSV/PDF).
- [ ] **E.5.4** End-to-end trial: one honest team, one "cheating" submission from another PC → alert within a minute.

## Done when
The trial raises the alert and the evidence is visible in the dashboard.

## Out of scope
Recordings viewer (H.5).
